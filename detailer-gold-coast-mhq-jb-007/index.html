Detailers, Gold Coast

Gold Coast, QLD · Casual and ongoing · Start: As needed

About the role

Location
Gold Coast, QLD
Position
Detailer
Type
Casual and ongoing
Start

We are looking for marine detailers to join the team caring for our managed yachts: washdowns, gelcoat and polish, stainless and teak care, canvas and clears, and coating work as booked. You would use our washdown routine and record your work and photos on each job. You would work on the motor yachts we look after on the Gold Coast, typically 40 to 120 ft, mostly berthed in Coomera and the surrounding marinas.

What we are looking for

  • Marine or automotive detailing experience; polishing and coating experience a plus
  • Attention to detail and pride in finish
  • Own transport and basic tools
  • Comfortable working on large vessels, at the berth and during yard periods

About Marine HQ

Who you would be working for

Marine HQ looks after motor yachts for their owners. We are based at The Boat Works in Coomera on the Gold Coast and work across the Gold Coast, Sydney and the Whitsundays, managing and maintaining yachts from 40 to 120 ft, supplying crew and relief skippers, and running deliveries.
