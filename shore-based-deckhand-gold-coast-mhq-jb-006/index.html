Shore-based deckhands keep our managed yachts in top condition at the berth: washdowns, deck and exterior care, lines and fenders, maintenance tasks from the worklist, yard-period support and helping move vessels around the marina. You would work on the motor yachts we look after on the Gold Coast, typically 40 to 120 ft, mostly berthed in Coomera and the surrounding marinas.

What we are looking for

  • Deck or marine trade experience preferred; keen newcomers with the right attitude considered
  • Own transport to Coomera and surrounding marinas
  • Reliable, hardworking and happy to follow a worklist
  • Current first aid or willing to obtain

Who you would be working for

Marine HQ looks after motor yachts for their owners. We are based at The Boat Works in Coomera on the Gold Coast and work across the Gold Coast, Sydney and the Whitsundays, managing and maintaining yachts from 40 to 120 ft, supplying crew and relief skippers, and running deliveries.
